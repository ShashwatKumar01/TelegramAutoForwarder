## 🚀 Key Features at a Glance

### 🛡️ 1. Restricted Content Bypass (Protected Channels)
- Forward posts from private channels or groups where **"Saving / Forwarding is Restricted"** (`noforwards` / copy protection).
- Automatically downloads and re-uploads photos, videos, voice notes, audio, and documents on the fly as clean, original uploads.
- Zero data traces: temporary files are purged from disk instantly.

### ✨ 2. Telegram Premium Emojis & Rich Formatting
- **Full Custom Emoji Retention**: Forwarded posts preserve animated Telegram Premium emojis exactly as in the source message!
- **Rich Typography**: Retains bold, italics, underlines, strikethroughs, spoiler blur, inline links, code blocks, and blockquotes.
- **Header & Footer Customization**: Seamlessly prepend or append custom footers (with dynamic tags like `{chat_title}`, `{date}`, `{time}`) while keeping all premium emoji entities fully intact.

### 🎨 3. Watermark Studio (Text & Logo PNG)
- Protect your brand and prevent content theft across social media!
- **Custom Text Watermark**: Add channel handles, website URLs, or custom text signatures.
- **Transparent PNG Logo Overlay**: Upload your custom brand logo and adjust scale, opacity, and positioning.
- Enable or disable watermarks independently for each forwarding pipeline rule.

### ⚡ 4. Real-Time Forwarding Engine (<300ms Latency)
- **Zero-Delay Relay**: Ultra-fast event-driven architecture processes and posts messages the millisecond they are published.
- **Clean Copy Mode**: Strips the "Forwarded from [Channel Name]" header so posts appear as your own original content.
- **Message Edit & Delete Sync**: Edits made in the source channel automatically sync to destination chats. Deleted messages can also be auto-deleted in destination channels.
- **Reply Thread Preservation**: Maintains replying threads across relayed messages.

### 🔍 5. Smart Filtering & Link Replacer
- **Keyword Whitelist / Blacklist**: Filter messages by specific keywords, comma-separated tags, or custom regex patterns.
- **Affiliate Tag Replacer**: Automatically find and replace Amazon (`tag=`), Flipkart, or Bitly referral links with your personal affiliate codes!
- **Source Mention & Link Stripper**: Automatically removes `@source_channel` mentions, t.me invite links, and unwanted bot links.
- **Media Type Filter**: Choose exactly which media types to relay: Photos, Videos, Documents, Audios, Voice Notes, Polls, or Text-only.

### ⏱️ 6. Auto-Delete & Ephemeral Deals
- Automatically delete forwarded messages after a specified duration (e.g. 5 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours).
- Ideal for **time-sensitive shopping deals**, **loot alerts**, and **temporary trading signals**.

### 📚 7. Historical Channel Cloner
- Backwards clone entire message archives from any channel from post #1 to the latest post (up to 10,000+ posts).
- Automatically inserts stylish date-separator headers (`📅 May 15, 2026`) between batches.
- Intelligent anti-flood pacing ensures your Telegram account remains 100% safe from Telegram rate-limits.

### 📱 8. Glassmorphic WebApp & Telegram Mini App
- Intuitive, mobile-responsive dashboard styled with modern glassmorphism.
- Manage rules, toggle pipelines, test watermarks, view active sessions, and check live dispatch activity logs with 1 tap.
- Open inside Telegram as a **Mini App** or in your web browser.

## 🛠️ Quick Start Guide (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Start the Bot
Open [@AutoForwarderTG_bot](https://t.me/AutoForwarderTG_bot) on Telegram and click **Start** (or send `/start`).

### Step 2: Connect Your Account
Tap **📱 Connect Telegram Account** and enter your phone number with your country code. Enter the login OTP code sent by Telegram.  
*(Your session string is encrypted with military-grade AES-256 Fernet encryption and stored securely).*

### Step 3: Create Your Forwarding Rule
Use the interactive Telegram bot menu or click **🌐 Open WebApp Dashboard**:
1. Select your **Source Channel** (any channel or group you are a member of).
2. Select your **Destination Channel** (where you want messages copied).
3. Configure your filters, watermarks, and headers/footers.
4. Hit **Save Pipeline** — your automated forwarder is now live!
